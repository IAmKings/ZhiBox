English: Black Holes

A black hole forms when matter collapses beyond its Schwarzschild radius, where escape velocity exceeds the speed of light. The first image of a black hole shadow, in galaxy M87, was released in 2019 by the Event Horizon Telescope, which combined radio dishes into an Earth-sized virtual aperture. Stellar-mass black holes weigh several times the sun, while supermassive ones reach billions of solar masses.
